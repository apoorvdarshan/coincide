# Provider marks

These small logos identify the CLI integrations in Coincide, an independent app.
They are not Coincide's app icon or branding. The marks belong to their respective
owners; their inclusion does not imply endorsement.

- OpenAI Blossom: unmodified black and white PNGs from the official
  [OpenAI logo download](https://cdn.openai.com/brand/OpenAI-Logos-2025.zip),
  retrieved 2026-10-24. See [OpenAI's design guidelines](https://openai.com/brand/).
- Claude: the unmodified 32px PNG favicon linked by [Claude](https://claude.com/),
  retrieved 2026-10-24 from
  `https://assets.claude.com/95a868946ac8a31e5ff832e2899f294aa368b836.png?w=32&h=32`.

Resources live in `Sources/Coincide/Resources/ProviderLogos` and are included in
both SwiftPM development builds and the packaged macOS app.
