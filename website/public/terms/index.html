Antigravity account risk

Google’s Antigravity terms call accessing the service with third-party software, tools or services a breach. Since February 2026 Google has suspended accounts for this, mostly personal Google AI Pro and Ultra sign-ins used through third-party tools, and a suspension also cuts off Gemini CLI and Code Assist. Coincide drives Google’s own Antigravity agent, but Google staff have given conflicting answers about tools that do.

Use Antigravity in Coincide at your own risk, and consider a separate Google account. Gemini API keys from Google AI Studio or Gemini Enterprise aren’t affected. Coincide asks you to acknowledge this once before you sign in or turn Antigravity on.

What agents do on your Mac

Agents in Coincide can run commands, create, change and delete files, use git (including commits, pushes and pull requests), use the browser in each thread, and, if you turn on Computer Use, operate other apps. In Full access mode they do this without asking first. Scheduled tasks send messages to agents on your behalf, and Keep Awake changes your Mac’s power settings.

You decide what agents may do and where. You’re responsible for reviewing their work, for what they do with your accounts and files, and for keeping backups. Use “Ask for approval” when you want to approve each command and file change.

Coincide Remote

A paired iPhone or iPad can control Coincide on that Mac, including sending messages to your agents. Pair only devices you control, and if one is lost, reset pairing in Coincide’s Remote settings.

Coincide Pro and tips

The Mac app is free. Coincide Remote needs Coincide Pro, an in-app subscription or one-time purchase, to use the app once a Mac is paired; pairing, restoring purchases, help and tips stay available without it. Coincide Remote also offers optional tips.

  • Through Apple. Purchases are made through the App Store and are also governed by Apple’s Media Services Terms and its Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Apple processes payment and handles refunds.
  • Subscriptions. Monthly and yearly Coincide Pro renew automatically at the price shown when you subscribed until you cancel. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase, or when a free trial ends, and each renewal is charged within the 24 hours before the current period ends. Cancel at least 24 hours before then to avoid the next charge, in your Apple Account’s subscription settings or with Manage Subscription in Coincide Remote. A free trial is offered once, to new subscribers.
  • Lifetime. Coincide Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase that unlocks Coincide Remote for as long as it’s offered.
  • Tips. Tips are a voluntary thank-you. They unlock nothing and aren’t restored on other devices.
  • Changes. Coincide Remote’s features and prices may change. A price change for a subscription follows Apple’s rules, including asking for your consent where they require it.

Moderation

Brulee removes spam it detects with fixed rules, such as mass mentions, the same message repeated in quick succession, or fake Nitro links. A first incident brings a 10-minute timeout, a second a 24-hour timeout, and a third a ban. Strikes reset after 30 days without a new one. Moderators may also remove content, time out or remove members for breaking these rules. To appeal, contact the server owner or email us.

Brulee, reports and AI answers

  • Reports are public. /bug and /feature create public issues in the apoorvdarshan/coincide GitHub repository, with your report quoted unchanged and, when available, an AI-organized summary. Anyone can read them, and they may stay public. Submit only what you’re happy to publish and have the right to share.
  • Limits. Reports share a wait that starts at 10 minutes, doubles with each further report up to a day, and resets after two days without one. /ask takes one question a minute.
  • AI answers can be wrong. /ask answers are written by AI from a short description of Coincide. Brulee can’t see your Mac, accounts or logs, remember earlier questions or take actions. Check important answers against the README.
  • Closed isn’t shipped. When an issue is closed, Brulee tells you, but that doesn’t mean a fix or feature is in a released version.
  • Brulee may be unavailable, change or stop at any time.

Please report security vulnerabilities privately, as described in SECURITY.md, never through Brulee or a public issue.
