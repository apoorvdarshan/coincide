Meet the cast.

Bots are standing assistants with a face, a personality and a job. Give one a morning routine and it shows up every weekday at nine.

Hover or tap one to say hello.

The rest of the job.

Agents outlive the app

A background host keeps every turn running when you quit or update Coincide. Open it again and the reply is still streaming.

Appshots

Press both ⌘ keys in any app. Coincide comes forward with that window attached, ready for your question.

Scheduled tasks

“Every Friday at six.” The prompt lands in the same thread, waits its turn if the agent is busy, and only runs once you approve it.

Voice

Dictate with Apple's on-device speech, a local Whisper model, or your own OpenAI or Groq key.

Computer Use

An agent clicks through native Mac apps with its own cursor, on any display, while you keep yours.

Keep Awake

Long jobs keep the Mac running, even on battery, and it sleeps again once the agents finish.

Bring your own agents.

Coincide drives each provider's own tool with your sign-in or API key. Nothing is resold or routed through anyone else.
